Your library, on your devices

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 September 2026

We do not operate a server that collects or stores your library. PostKeeper processes saved content on your device. If you choose Google Drive sync, encrypted copies are stored in your own Google account—not in a maintainer-controlled library service.

This does not mean that no data exists or that no provider processes information. Your device stores your library, Google handles optional authorization and sync, GitHub hosts the website, and support issues are public.

1. Who and what this policy covers

This policy covers the PostKeeper web app at keep.fishese.cc and its companion browser extensions and Android app. “We” means the maintainer of the fishese/postkeeper project. Independently hosted or modified copies may have different operators and policies.

PostKeeper is a development preview. This policy describes implemented features; a self-hosted sync service remains planned.

2. Data stored and used on your device

To provide offline reading, search, organization, and synchronization, PostKeeper stores saved page text and HTML snapshots, images, titles, source URLs, categories, reading flags, capture diagnostics, search indexes, device/library identifiers, and sync history in browser storage (IndexedDB and, where available, OPFS). The app shell is cached by a service worker.

Local library data is not encrypted by PostKeeper at rest. Protection depends on your device, browser profile, operating system, and who can access them.

Portable backups are downloaded only after you explicitly choose plaintext export. They contain saved content, URLs, categories, snapshots, and images, and are readable without your recovery key. They exclude Google credentials, browser session storage, and transient logs. Printing and PDF saving also create copies of your saved content. Protect these files separately; PostKeeper does not upload them to its static host.

Local diagnostics can be reviewed before download. They include aggregate counts, device storage usage, browser and processing versions, and sync status, while omitting article content, URLs, credentials, and logs. Sharing a downloaded diagnostic file is your choice.

When you explicitly capture a page, the extension reads its rendered content and fetches relevant assets using browser permissions and the existing website session. The website can see these ordinary requests. PostKeeper does not request your website password or send session cookies to a library server. Captured pages can still contain personal information or sensitive links; choose what you save carefully.

The extension keeps a bounded local transfer queue until the app acknowledges an import. Transfers expire after 30 minutes; expired records are removed when queue maintenance next runs, not necessarily while the browser is closed.

Shared URLs initially create pending links, not archived pages. An installed compatible PWA handles shared POST data locally in its service worker. The Android app queues shared text in its private storage until the local library acknowledges it. No shared page URL or content is sent to the static host by these flows.

The Android capture browser stores cookies and website storage in separate site-session profiles on your device. You sign in directly on the website; requests go to that website as in an ordinary browser. Website JavaScript has no PostKeeper native bridge. Explicit capture reads a credential-scrubbed page snapshot and retrieves available same-origin images with that profile's session. Temporary capture files live in private app cache. Clear this site or Clear all browsing data removes capture sessions without deleting your saved library. The Android library is separate from your browser/PWA library.

You may explicitly save a recovery-key convenience copy in the Android app. It is encrypted with Android Keystore and stored outside system and portable backups. Loading or removing it requires a native confirmation; it is not protected by a separate biometric prompt. Keep an independent recovery copy. Removing the app or losing device keys can make this copy unavailable. The Android preview does not perform embedded Google authorization; use the browser/PWA for Drive sync.

3. Optional Google Drive authorization and sync

Google is contacted when you select Load Google sign-in. Authorization then occurs through Google. You can use the local library without a Google account or Drive connection.

PostKeeper requests only https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata. It uses this permission to list, read, create, and synchronize PostKeeper objects in the hidden application-data folder. It does not request access to unrelated Drive files, Gmail, or your contacts, and does not request profile information for a PostKeeper account.

Sync encrypts article contents and library metadata on your device before upload. Google stores encrypted content, encrypted operations, and a recovery-key-wrapped master key. Google can still observe your account, network requests, object identifiers, sizes, and timing. Decryption and search take place on your device.

Google access tokens and unwrapped encryption keys remain in memory for the current app session; PostKeeper does not persist a refresh token or send these secrets to the static website host. A recovery key you copy elsewhere is under your control. We do not hold a recovery copy and cannot reconstruct a lost key.

Google user data is used only to provide the sync and restore features you request. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, transfer it to data brokers, or use it to train AI models. PostKeeper's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

4. Hosting, cookies, and support

GitHub Pages serves the app files. Like other web hosts, GitHub processes technical request information, such as IP addresses, browser information, requested paths, and timestamps, for operating and protecting its services. PostKeeper does not send saved article contents, recovery keys, or Google tokens to that host.

PostKeeper includes no advertising, analytics, session replay, automatic crash reporting, or tracking cookies. Browser storage is used for app functionality. Google and GitHub may use their own cookies and process data under their policies when you interact with their services. They may process information outside your country.

If you voluntarily open an issue, your GitHub identity, message, and attachments are available to the maintainers and the public. We use that information to respond to questions and maintain the project; it remains subject to GitHub's retention and deletion controls.

See Google's Privacy Policy and GitHub's Privacy Statement for their practices.

5. Retention, revocation, and deletion

  • Local copies: use your browser's site-data controls for the exact PostKeeper origin to remove its local library and caches. Remove extension data separately through your browser. Uninstalling a PWA alone may not clear site data. Clearing one device does not erase other devices or Drive.
  • Google access: use Disconnect Google Drive to request revocation, or remove access through Google Account connections. Disconnecting does not delete local or remote library data.
  • Remote copies: use Google Drive's application-management controls to delete PostKeeper's hidden app data. Stop sync on every device first; a device with retained data and authorization can upload it again. Deleting hidden app data can be irreversible and removes the remote recovery copy.
  • History: the current sync format retains operations, snapshot versions, and deletion markers. Removing something from a visible view is not a secure erasure of its history. Automatic history purging and an in-app remote wipe are not currently provided.

Retention of local copies depends on your actions and browser storage policies; browsers can also evict data. Google controls retention of data in its systems. We cannot delete copies on your devices or in your Google account on your behalf.

6. Security and policy changes

HTTPS, encrypted sync, and an isolated reader reduce risks but do not guarantee security or prevent every loss. An unlocked device, malicious extension, compromised software, or exposed recovery key can compromise your library.

We will update this page and its date when practices change. Material changes to Google data use will be disclosed in the app and any required new consent obtained before that new use begins. This policy is not consent to undisclosed uses.

7. Questions and privacy requests

Contact the maintainer through the PostKeeper issue tracker. Issues are public. Do not post passwords, tokens, recovery keys, saved articles, or other private information. For a sensitive concern, ask for a private contact method without including the sensitive details.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights concerning your personal information. We can address information you have provided to the project, but do not possess your local library or encrypted Drive account data. Direct provider-held data requests to Google or GitHub as appropriate.
